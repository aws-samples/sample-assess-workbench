# Welcome to Assess Workbench

Assess Workbench gives you structured, traceable assessments of the documents that drive business, risk, and compliance decisions. A team of specialist AI agents reviews each document against your standards, frameworks, and policies — producing findings, recommendations, and remediation that map back to the source evidence.

## What It Does

For every project, Assess Workbench builds a **custom review workflow**. It analyses your documents and selects the most relevant specialists to conduct the assessment. The agents are completely customisable, and you can create new ones for your own domain.

**Included specialists:**

- **Architecture** — evaluates quality attributes (performance, reliability, security, maintainability)
- **Security** — maps findings to OWASP Top 10 and CWE
- **Risk** — uses a likelihood × consequence matrix aligned to ISO 31000
- **Compliance** — assesses against your regulatory and policy corpus

You stay in control the whole way through:

- **You shape the plan.** Before any agent runs, you see the proposed review strategy and can refine it — add or remove agents, adjust depth and focus areas, and decide where to apply extra quality coaching.
- **Quality is scored, not assumed.** A quality judge scores every agent's output for completeness, specificity, and actionability. An optional coaching loop feeds critiques back to the agent to iteratively improve weak results.
- **Findings are traceable.** Each finding, recommendation, and remediation step links back to the document sections and standards that informed it, so you can verify the reasoning and follow the evidence chain.
- **You can interrogate the reasoning.** Chat directly with any specialist agent to explore why it raised a finding or to ask follow-up questions, with full access to the findings and the source document.

## Your Standards, Your Knowledge

Agents ground their findings in a standards corpus that you control — not the open internet. You decide what's in it: international frameworks, regulator commentary, internal policies, or newly released guidance the AI model has never seen. You manage the corpus through the admin interface, and updates take effect immediately.

This means you know exactly where every recommendation comes from — an authoritative source you've approved.

See [Organizational Contexts](/docs/contexts) for how to tailor assessments further to your specific business environment.

## How It Works

1. **Create a project** and upload the documents you want assessed.
2. **A planner proposes a workflow** — which agents to run, how deeply, and what to focus on.
3. **You review and approve the plan**, adjusting anything you want before execution.
4. **Specialist agents assess your documents**, grounded in your reference corpus of standards.
5. **A quality judge scores each agent's output**, with optional coaching to refine results.
6. **You explore the results** — browse traceable findings, watch the review unfold live, and chat with any agent for a deeper dive.

## What Can I Assess?

Assess Workbench isn't tied to one domain. Because the workflow and agents adapt to each project, the same workbench can assess:

- **Technical documents** — concept papers, solution designs, architecture proposals
- **Business documents** — policies, committee charters, operating procedures
- **Governance artifacts** — existing risk assessments, compliance attestations, control narratives

If a document needs expert review against a known set of standards, Assess Workbench can help you do it faster, more consistently, and with a clear trail back to the evidence.
